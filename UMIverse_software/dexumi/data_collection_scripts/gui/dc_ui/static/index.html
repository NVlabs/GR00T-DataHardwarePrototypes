STEP 01

Calibrate gloves

Each glove needs a one-time calibration so its finger encoders report correct joint angles. You can skip this step if your gloves are already calibrated.

Calibrate a new glove

  1. Plug in only the glove to calibrate: Unplug every other glove. The boards look identical over USB, so a glove can only be recognised when it is plugged in alone.
  2. Seat it in the calibration tool. Place the glove fully in the calibration tool, with every finger in its slot and none bouncing back up.
  3. Choose the left or right glove below, based on the hand it is worn on.
  4. Record the pose. When the status says it is ready, keep the glove still and press Record pose, then check the result and press Save calibration.
  5. Repeat for any other glove that needs it.

How to identify cameras
Click Identify cameras. Show the on-screen board to each hand's iPhone and wrist OAK together, left hand first, then right. Check the live previews before scanning. Closing the dialog releases the cameras. You can also select cameras manually from the lists; the head OAK is optional.

      The recorder opens its preview window on the workstation's desktop. Focus that window: press s to record, w to save, a to discard, t to set the task, q to quit. Stop here sends Ctrl-C: an episode still being recorded is discarded, and alignments still running are interrupted until this folder is started again (q waits for them).
      

      


      
  
      STEP 05
      Convert for training
      
  
      When a session is finished, convert it to a LeRobot dataset: the glove data and fingertip forces become robot-hand joint positions and torques, then the dataset is written to <session>_lerobot next to the session. A session converts once every episode is aligned, and not while the recorder runs. You can also convert later from a terminal: ./launch.sh lerobot --input-dataset output/<session>_aligned.
